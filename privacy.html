Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

This policy applies to the apps SDA Fundamental Beliefs and SDA Beliefs - Imani za Msingi, developed by Almodad Amos Yaragwiller. It explains what information the apps collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password when you register, or your name, email and photo from Google when you sign in with Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as your name, church and profile photo.
  • Content you share in Brethren: posts, comments, likes, messages, friend requests and photos.

Information collected automatically

  • Reading progress, points, streaks and badges, saved to your account so they follow you across devices.
  • A notification token, so the app can send you reminders and messages.
  • Your device's advertising ID and basic device information, used by Google AdMob to show ads.

Your preferences, such as language, text size and reminder time, stay on your device.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your progress in sync.
  • To show your profile and posts to other Brethren members, according to the visibility you choose.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have turned on.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. It is processed only by the services that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage and notifications).
  • Google AdMob (advertising).

Other members can see what you post in Brethren and your public profile, depending on your visibility settings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, profile photos, reading progress and everything you wrote in Brethren, then deletes the account itself. You can also request deletion here.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts it in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions?

Email modaofhope@gmail.com

Sera ya Faragha

Imesasishwa 15 Oktoba 2026

Sera hii inahusu programu za SDA Fundamental Beliefs na SDA Beliefs - Imani za Msingi, zilizotengenezwa na Almodad Amos Yaragwiller. Inaeleza taarifa ambazo programu zinakusanya, jinsi zinavyotumika, na machaguo uliyonayo.

Taarifa unazotupa

  • Taarifa za akaunti: barua pepe na nenosiri unapojisajili, au jina, barua pepe na picha kutoka Google unapoingia kwa Google. Nenosiri linashughulikiwa na Firebase Authentication na hatulioni kamwe.
  • Taarifa za wasifu unazochagua kuongeza, kama jina, kanisa na picha ya wasifu.
  • Maudhui unayoshiriki kwa Wajoli: machapisho, maoni, vipendwa, jumbe, maombi ya urafiki na picha.

Taarifa zinazokusanywa zenyewe

  • Maendeleo ya usomaji, pointi, mfululizo na tuzo, huhifadhiwa kwenye akaunti yako ili zikufuate kwenye vifaa vyako vyote.
  • Tokeni ya arifa, ili programu ikutumie vikumbusho na jumbe.
  • Kitambulisho cha matangazo cha kifaa chako na taarifa za msingi za kifaa, vinavyotumiwa na Google AdMob kuonyesha matangazo.

Mapendeleo yako, kama lugha, ukubwa wa herufi na muda wa kikumbusho, hubaki kwenye kifaa chako.

Jinsi tunavyozitumia

  • Kuendesha akaunti yako na kuweka maendeleo yako sawa kila mahali.
  • Kuonyesha wasifu na machapisho yako kwa wanachama wengine wa Wajoli, kulingana na mwonekano uliouchagua.
  • Kukutumia vikumbusho na arifa ulizowasha.
  • Kuonyesha matangazo yanayosaidia programu kuendelea kuwa bure.

Tunashirikiana nani

Hatuuzi taarifa zako binafsi. Zinashughulikiwa tu na huduma zinazowezesha programu kufanya kazi:

  • Google Firebase (kuingia, hifadhidata, uhifadhi wa faili na arifa).
  • Google AdMob (matangazo).

Wanachama wengine wanaweza kuona unachochapisha kwa Wajoli na wasifu wako wa umma, kulingana na mipangilio yako ya mwonekano.

Kufuta akaunti yako

Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako wakati wowote ndani ya programu kupitia Mipangilio → Futa akaunti. Hii inaondoa kabisa wasifu wako, picha za wasifu, maendeleo ya usomaji na kila ulichoandika kwa Wajoli, kisha inafuta akaunti yenyewe. Unaweza pia kuomba kufutwa hapa.

Watoto

Programu hizi hazijalengwa kwa watoto walio chini ya miaka 13, na hatukusanyi taarifa zao kwa kujua.

Usalama

Taarifa zako zinahifadhiwa na Google Firebase, inayozisimba wakati wa kusafirishwa na zikiwa zimehifadhiwa. Hakuna mfumo ulio salama kikamilifu, kwa hiyo tafadhali tumia nenosiri imara.

Mabadiliko ya sera hii

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii. Tarehe iliyo juu inaonyesha toleo la karibuni.
