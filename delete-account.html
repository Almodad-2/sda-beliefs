Delete Your Account

SDA Fundamental Beliefs · SDA Beliefs - Imani za Msingi

These apps are developed by Almodad Amos Yaragwiller. You can delete your account and its data at any time, either in the app or by email.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or your Google account if you signed in with Google.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your profile and profile photos.
  • Your reading progress, points, streaks and badges.
  • Everything you wrote in Brethren: posts, comments, likes, messages and friend requests.
  • Your sign-in account (email or Google sign-in).

What is kept

We keep none of your account data after deletion. Settings stored only on your phone, such as language and text size, stay until you uninstall the app. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Futa Akaunti Yako

SDA Fundamental Beliefs · SDA Beliefs - Imani za Msingi

Programu hizi zimetengenezwa na Almodad Amos Yaragwiller. Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako na taarifa zake wakati wowote, ndani ya programu au kwa barua pepe.

Futa ndani ya programu (njia ya haraka)

  1. Fungua programu na uingie.
  2. Nenda Mipangilio kisha shuka hadi chini.
  3. Gusa Futa akaunti.
  4. Thibitisha kwa nenosiri lako, au akaunti yako ya Google kama uliingia kwa Google.

Akaunti na taarifa zako zinafutwa papo hapo.

Au omba kufutwa kwa barua pepe

Kama huwezi kutumia programu, tutumie barua pepe kutoka anwani uliyoingia nayo na tutafuta akaunti yako ndani ya siku 30.

Tuma ombi la kufuta

Kinachofutwa

  • Wasifu wako na picha za wasifu.
  • Maendeleo yako ya usomaji, pointi, mfululizo na tuzo.
  • Kila ulichoandika kwa Wajoli: machapisho, maoni, vipendwa, jumbe na maombi ya urafiki.
  • Akaunti yako ya kuingia (barua pepe au Google).

Kinachobaki

Hatuhifadhi taarifa zozote za akaunti yako baada ya kufutwa. Mipangilio iliyo kwenye simu yako pekee, kama lugha na ukubwa wa herufi, hubaki hadi utakapoondoa programu. Taarifa za matangazo zinazokusanywa na Google AdMob zinashughulikiwa chini ya Sera ya Faragha ya Google.
